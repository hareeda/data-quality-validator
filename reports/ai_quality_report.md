**Business Data Quality Validation Report**

**Executive Summary:**

This report summarizes the results of a data quality validation exercise, which identified 14 data quality issues across 15 records. The majority of issues (9) are classified as medium severity, while 5 are classified as high severity. The findings highlight several key areas for improvement, including potential data quality risks that require immediate attention.

**Main Data Quality Risks:**

1. **Unusual Annual Growth:** The data contains 1 record with an unusual annual growth rate. This requires further review to determine its accuracy and potential impact on business decisions.
2. **Potential Duplicate Company:** The data contains 6 records with potential duplicate company information. This requires review to confirm the accuracy of company names and addresses.
3. **Missing Value and Invalid Country:** The data contains 2 records with missing values and 1 record with an invalid country code. These issues require attention to ensure data completeness and consistency.
4. **Invalid Credit Score and Negative Revenue:** The data contains 2 records with invalid credit scores and 1 record with negative revenue. These issues require review to ensure data accuracy and compliance with business rules.

**Recommended Corrective Actions, Ordered by Priority:**

1. Review and validate company information to resolve potential duplicates.
2. Investigate and correct unusual annual growth rates.
3. Update missing values and correct invalid country codes.
4. Review and correct invalid credit scores and negative revenue.

**Limitations and Items Requiring Human Review:**

* The data quality validation exercise did not capture all potential issues, and additional review is required to ensure data completeness and accuracy.
* Human review is necessary to confirm the accuracy of unusual growth rates and potential duplicate company information.
* Further investigation is required to determine the root cause of invalid credit scores and negative revenue.

Please note that this report highlights data quality issues and recommended corrective actions, but does not claim that the data has been corrected.
